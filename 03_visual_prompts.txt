HOW TO ASSEMBLE EACH PROMPT
======================================================================

For every generated shot, submit the complete MASTER PREFIX, then the relevant identity/environment lock, then the SCENE BLOCK, and finish with MASTER NEGATIVE. These are reusable locked components, not optional suggestions. Do not ask the model to render captions, citations, interface labels or philosophical terminology; all typography is added in post-production.

For a storyboard duration longer than the model's native clip length, generate the first clean clip, choose the strongest end frame, then use Extend or Frames-to-Video for the remaining time. Do not slow a clip below 80% merely to fill duration. Generate one candidate first. Generate a second candidate only when the first fails anatomy, action, continuity, evidence labeling space or camera intent.
